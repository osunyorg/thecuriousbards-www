Exploring 18th-century Gaelic culture.

body: >-


This programme invites you to explore the Gaelic music of 18th-century Ireland and Scotland through tunes drawn from collections of the time. These collections offer a rare glimpse into musical life in both countries, preserving a wealth of melodies that extends well beyond the dance repertoire. The lilting rhythms of Irish jigs find their match in the spirited drive of Scottish reels, while ballads draw us into their stories and laments offer moments of quiet reflection. The Curious Bards bring a researcher's curiosity and a performer's energy to this music, inviting you to discover it with them. Each piece brings a discovery, and every contrast offers a fresh way to hear the music, from the intimacy of a melody to the whirl of a wild dance!
