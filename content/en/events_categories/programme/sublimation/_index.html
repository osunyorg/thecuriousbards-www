body: >-


This new programme shines a light on the little-known music of Norway and Sweden. With instruments new to the ensemble, including the Swedish nyckelharpa and the Norwegian Hardanger fiddle, The Curious Bards reveal the richness of a repertoire whose strikingly individual melodies and harmonies bear the influence of Gaelic culture. The dances and songs were all composed in the 18th century and survive in manuscripts that have rarely been explored. A programme full of unfamiliar sounds, utterly distinctive and wonderfully fresh!
