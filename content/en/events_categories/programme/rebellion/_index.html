body: >-


In this new programme, The Curious Bards explore the close ties between popular and art music in the work of Henry Purcell, one of the great English composers of the 17th century. His secular music leaves little doubt that he knew the popular tunes of his day. Their influence is particularly clear in his gift for writing dances, both for his operas and for the incidental music he composed for plays. That same influence can be heard in some of Purcell's airs and many of his songs, performed here by mezzo-soprano Ilektra Platiopoulou. With this slight shift towards art music, The Curious Bards offer a fresh perspective on a remarkably versatile composer whose genius for writing in a popular idiom is unmistakable.
