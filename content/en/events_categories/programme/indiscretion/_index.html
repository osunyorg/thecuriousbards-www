After their first exploration of Gaelic instrumental music, The Curious Bards let their curiosity lead them a little further. This time, they dare to be a little more indiscreet! This programme takes us into less familiar corners of 18th-century Irish and Scottish music, each with a character of its own. Ballads sit alongside the distinctive rhythms of hornpipes, the swirling motion of slip jigs and the sharp rhythmic contrasts of strathspeys. The voice opens new doors, too. Songs in Gaelic and drinking songs let us hear the words behind the tunes, from a confidence shared to the conviviality of a drink together. As we listen, the character of each piece comes more clearly into focus, and the music reveals a more intimate side. An invitation to listen closely, draw a little nearer and let curiosity turn into indiscretion!
