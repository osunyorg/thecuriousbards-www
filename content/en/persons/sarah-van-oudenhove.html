Viola da gamba & Cello

body: >-


In Sarah Van Oudenhove's hands, the viola da gamba has an adventurous spirit. An inventive and enthusiastic musician, she brings exacting standards to her playing, along with a delight in making music that is infectious. She loves to explore, experiment and share what she discovers. That curiosity shapes both her approach to early music and her love of traditional music. Sarah began playing the viol at the age of six, studying with Christian Sala at the conservatoire in Perpignan. She continued her studies with Coen Engelhard in Toulouse before joining Marianne Muller's class at the CNSMD in Lyon in 2003, graduating in 2008. Along the way, workshops and encounters with Jérôme Hantaï, Wieland Kuijken, Christophe Coin and Jordi Savall helped shape her playing. Since then, she has performed and recorded regularly with ensembles including L'Achéron, Les Sonadori, La Chapelle Rhénane, Pygmalion, Le Poème Harmonique, Clematis, Ensemble Gilles Binchois, Cappella Mediterranea and the Chœur de chambre de Namur. She works with precision, listens closely to her fellow musicians and delights in shaping a performance together, bringing both vitality and generosity to the music. Sarah also likes to take her viol off the beaten track. Her love of folk music has led her to explore how early repertoire can be played on the viola da gamba. How can a player recapture the music's rhythmic drive, its accents, its freedom? These questions give her room to experiment, drawing on both her knowledge of historical styles and her experience of playing traditional music. These two passions find a natural home in The Curious Bards. Sarah brings imagination, a gift for playing with others and a sparkling energy to the ensemble, all while paying close attention to every musical detail. For her, the care that goes into the work goes hand in hand with the joy of playing together.
