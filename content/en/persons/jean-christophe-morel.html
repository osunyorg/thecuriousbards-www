Renaissance Cittern, Baroque Cittern & Baroque violin

body: >-


From violin to cittern, guitar to mandolin, Jean-Christophe Morel enjoys finding new ways to make music. Drawing on his classical training, Irish musical traditions and improvisation, he brings precision, spontaneity and a spirit of exploration to his playing. He began studying the violin with Robert Papavrami at the École Pierre Rode, then joined Manuel Solans's class at the Bordeaux Conservatoire in 2001. In 2009, he entered the CNSMD de Lyon, where he continued his studies with Marie Charvet, Claire Bernard and Nicolas Gourbeix. Irish music gradually came to play a central role in his musical life. An Erasmus exchange in Dublin during the first year of his master's degree gave him the chance to immerse himself in the repertoire and deepen his understanding of how it is played. Playing in an oral tradition, learning tunes by ear and improvising became sources of inspiration, leading him to explore new rhythms, different ways of shaping a phrase and other instruments. That curiosity has taken him into several musical worlds. He performs with The Jeremiahs, Onde and The Curious Bards, while continuing to play violin with orchestras including the Orchestre national Bordeaux Aquitaine and the Orchestre national du Capitole de Toulouse. With The Curious Bards, his command of both bowed and plucked strings shapes his inventive approach to the repertoire. On violin and cittern alike, he experiments with tone colour, rhythm and different ways of accompanying his fellow musicians. His imagination and the freedom of his playing bring a distinctive voice to the ensemble's musical conversation.
