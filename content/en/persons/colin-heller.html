Kontrabasharpa & Baroque violin

body: >-


From the baroque violin to the Norwegian Hardanger fiddle, from the piano to the Swedish nyckelharpa, he enjoys discovering music by switching instruments. His curiosity takes him from early music and folk traditions to jazz and rock, bringing him into contact with musicians from many different backgrounds. Born in Briançon, he began playing the violin and piano at the age of five and later discovered the organ and the baroque violin. After studying at the Darius Milhaud Conservatoire in Aix-en-Provence, he joined the baroque violin class at the CNSMD de Lyon in 2018. Alongside his studies, he taught himself to play the mandolin, nyckelharpa, gittern, Hardanger fiddle and viola d'amore. Each new instrument brought a different sound, a new set of techniques and another repertoire to explore. From 2012 to 2014, he played in the Mediterranean Youth Orchestra under the batons of Alain Altinoglu, François-Xavier Roth and Gianandrea Noseda. Orchestral playing is one of the many ways he has pursued the pleasure of making music with others. His collaborations have brought him together with traditional musicians Patrick Vaillant, Françoise Atlan, Moneim Adwan, Gabriel Lenoir, Daniel Muringer and Manu Théron, as well as with jazz musicians Fabrizio Cassol and Jasser Haj Youssef. He has also shared the stage with baroque musicians Monique Zanetti, Lila Hajosi and Dominique Serve, and with the band Deluxe. His work with Didier Rousselet and Côme de Bellescize has taken him into the theatre. What connects these experiences? The pleasure of learning from others and discovering what music they might make together. The many instruments he plays give him just as many ways to experiment, invent and share what he discovers. With The Curious Bards, his love of unusual instruments and traditional music finds a natural home. From the violin to the nyckelharpa, he brings new colours to the ensemble's sound as they explore Scandinavian repertoires together. It is the sheer pleasure of playing together that feeds his curiosity and sparks new ideas.
