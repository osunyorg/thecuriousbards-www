Baroque Violin & Artistic Director

body: >-


Violinist and founder of The Curious Bards, Alix Boivert explores the connections between baroque music and popular traditions. His love of research and forgotten repertoire takes him back to the original sources, and it is on stage, in the energy and spontaneity of live performance, that his discoveries come to life. Alix studied with Robert Papavrami and later at the Bordeaux Conservatoire. He discovered the harpsichord and the baroque violin before joining Odile Édouard's class at the CNSMD de Lyon, where he earned his master's degree in 2014. He has appeared as a soloist in major concert halls across Europe and has worked with ensembles including Le Concert Spirituel, Pygmalion, Correspondances, Amarillis and La Chapelle Rhénane. Drawn to the traditional music of Ireland and Scotland, he began exploring the repertoire by ear and independently before spending a year in Dublin to deepen his understanding of the playing traditions. In 2015, he founded The Curious Bards, with whom he brings the popular music of 17th- and 18th-century northern Europe back to life. The ensemble has given more than 300 concerts and released three albums on harmonia mundi. A fourth album, Rebellion! , devoted to Henry Purcell and featuring a guest appearance by Philippe Katerine, is due for release in January 2027. Alix is also developing Íorna , a stage production that brings together music, song and dance to tell the story of a weaver in 18th-century Ireland, and Évocation , a programme exploring Irish and Scottish airs arranged by Haydn and Beethoven. His research now extends to the music heard in the United States in the late 18th and early 19th centuries, as part of a residency project with Villa Albertine.
