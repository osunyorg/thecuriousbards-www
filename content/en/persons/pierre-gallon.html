Harpsichord & pianoforte

body: >-


In Pierre Gallon's hands, the harpsichord tells stories. His programmes bring works from the Renaissance to the present day into conversation, pairing pieces in unexpected ways and inviting us to hear them afresh. Curious and inventive, he finds in the harpsichord the freedom to develop a musical voice that draws on historical research and speaks with sensitivity and warmth. Pierre grew up in a house with instruments in every corner and discovered the pleasure of playing at an early age. After studying with Bibiane Lapointe and Thierry Maeder, he continued his studies at the CNSMD de Paris with Olivier Baumont and Blandine Rannou. Encounters with Blandine Verlet, Élisabeth Joyé and Pierre Hantaï had a lasting influence on him and opened up new ways of thinking about the harpsichord. For more than ten years, he has worked closely with Raphaël Pichon and the ensemble Pygmalion, both in concert and on record. As a soloist, he has appeared at La Roque d'Anthéron, the Festival de Saintes, the Utrecht Early Music Festival and the Tage Alter Musik in Regensburg. His recordings reflect his taste for exploring less familiar territory. Each invites the listener into a different world, from his first album, devoted to Pierre Attaingnant in 2014, to After Dark , released by harmonia mundi, which explores the music of the night in Elizabethan England. His recordings have earned several Diapasons d'Or, ffff ratings from Télérama , a Choc and several Coups de cœur from Classica , as well as recommendations from France Musique and France Culture. A devoted chamber musician, Pierre has built lasting artistic partnerships with Bertrand Cuiller, Lucile Boulanger, Sophie Gent and the writer Pascal Quignard. With The Curious Bards, he shares the pleasure of bringing different repertoires together and letting the instruments speak to one another. His imagination at the keyboard enriches the ensemble's explorations and reflects a conviction that runs through all his work: music is, above all, an art of conversation.
