Recorder & cornetto

body: >-


From recorder to cornetto, Sarah Dubus explores a rich palette of tone colours and expressive possibilities. As a performer and teacher, she divides her time between the concert stage, ensemble projects and teaching, all of which reflect her love of musical dialogue. She began studying the recorder with Marie Hervé at the Rouen Conservatoire before continuing with Jean-Pierre Nicolas at the CRD de Paris-Saclay, where the jury unanimously awarded her the Diplôme d'études musicales . At the CNSMD de Lyon, she studied cornetto with Jean Tubéry and recorder with Pierre Hamon. She earned a master's degree in each instrument, receiving top marks and the unanimous congratulations of the jury for both. She also holds a bachelor's degree in musicology. In 2016, she won first prize for solo cornetto at the Toulouse International Early Brass Competition. Sarah has performed at numerous festivals across Europe, as well as in Japan and Mexico. She has appeared with La Fenice, Correspondances, Pygmalion, Collegium 1704, Cappella Cracoviensis, Les Meslanges, Les Traversées baroques, La Tempête, InAlto, Scherzi Musicali and Concerto Soave. She also co-founded Ozio Regio, reflecting her commitment to chamber music and to developing projects with fellow musicians. Teaching is central to her musical life. Sarah holds the French certificat d'aptitude and a master's degree in teaching. She has taught both recorder and cornett at the CRD de Paris-Saclay, helping students discover the range of possibilities each instrument offers. With The Curious Bards, Sarah draws on both instruments to explore new connections between early music and popular traditions. In Rebellion! , a programme devoted to Purcell and the popular music that inspired him, the distinctive voices of the recorder and cornetto mingle with the strings, the singing and the rhythms of the dances.
