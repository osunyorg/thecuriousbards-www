body: >-


Dancer, teacher, activist and now DJ, Marine Wroniszewski, known as Easzy, loves bringing people and disciplines together. From dance battles to the stage, and through her teaching and the events she organises, she champions an intuitive approach to dance that blends styles and draws on what makes each dancer unique. Travel, study and encounters with other artists have shaped her work, which draws on both hip-hop culture and formal dance training. In 2014, she moved to Bordeaux to train as a hip-hop performer. She went on to work with the companies Dyptik, Chriki'z, 1 Des Si, Hors Série, Androphyne, Les Petites Secousses and Caliband Théâtre. In 2024, she co-choreographed Le Bal des Judoka for the company Kaminari. How does who we are shape the way we dance? This question lies at the heart of Marine's work. She explores the relationship between the person and the artist, making room for reflection, intuition and the many sides of each performer. The same approach informs her teaching. She teaches on professional dance programmes and leads workshops in mainland France, on Réunion Island and abroad, with a particular focus on stage presence and the commitment dancers bring to each movement. Community is just as important to her. In 2015, she co-founded Fish & Shoes in Bordeaux, an association through which she organises events that bring the dance community together. In 2022, she joined the team behind Radio Biche , a programme exploring urban culture on La Clé des Ondes. Two years later, she took to the decks as Leeloo Dallas, finding another way to share music and get people moving. With The Curious Bards, Marine is part of Íorna , a production that brings dance, voice and period instruments together. She brings her own choreographic language and her sensitivity to the individual performer to the production, exploring how a gesture can carry the music further and bring the story to life.
