Our story

Since 2015, The Curious Bards have brought together musicians who share a passion for the popular music of northern Europe in the 17th and 18th centuries. Their approach is a little out of the ordinary, combining in-depth musicological research with boundless energy. Trained at conservatoires in Lyon, Paris and Basel, the musicians have immersed themselves in the music of Ireland, Scotland, England, Sweden and Norway, rediscovering early repertoires and bringing their own creative, contemporary touch to the way they play them. They're also a band of slightly eccentric researchers, always ready to explore unfamiliar sounds, piece together long-forgotten playing techniques and bring old tunes back to life with infectious enthusiasm. Their sound? A blend of authenticity, scholarship and raw energy that gets you dancing as much as it gets you thinking. Each concert is a journey where tradition and innovation meet, and the music takes on a thrilling life of its own. The Cité de la Voix in Vézelay supported The Curious Bards from the outset. In 2016, the ensemble joined the Ambronay Festival's EEEmerging programme, and since 2020, the Caisse des Dépôts has been its principal patron. Across more than 300 concerts and four albums for Harmonia Mundi ( Extradition , 2017, Indiscretion , 2023, Sublimation , 2025, and Rebellion , due out in January 2027), The Curious Bards have made this approach their own, pairing rigorous scholarship with unbridled creativity. It's tradition reimagined with a touch of madness, and the result is as thoughtful as it is joyful!
